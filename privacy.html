Focus Buddy privacy

Focus Buddy is a Chrome extension that helps you stay on task. This page is the privacy policy for Chrome Web Store review and for people who install it.

What we read on your device

  • During lock-in, the active tab’s URL, title, and a short page excerpt so we can decide on-task vs distracted. That log stays in Chrome storage on your computer unless you use friends features that you turn on.
  • Task text, timer state, folders, and your chosen bunny or cat.

What we send to other services

  • Firebase (Google): email/password account, public profile fields, friends, optional now-playing if you connect Spotify and enable sharing.
  • Google Gemini: only if you paste your own API key in Settings. Then the task plus the current tab URL/title/excerpt may be sent to Google to classify the tab. No key means Gemini is not used.
  • Spotify: only if you click Connect Spotify. We request currently-playing playback state.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not collect passwords for other sites.
  • Private session details stay on your device if cloud sync fails.

You can remove the extension at any time from chrome://extensions.
